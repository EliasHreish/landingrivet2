The RIVET software service for running a gym, including its web and mobile applications, reminders and reports.
Customer
The gym, company or sole trader that signs a subscription agreement.
Users
The owners, managers and staff the Customer authorises to use its account.
Members
The people whose details the Customer keeps in the Platform: members, trial visitors and leads.
