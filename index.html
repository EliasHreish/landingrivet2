Why RIVET

Most gyms run on a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp group and someone's memory.

Sales sit in one place, payments in another, and the front desk in a notebook. When something slips, nobody can say who was responsible. RIVET puts the whole operation in one system, where every action has a record and a name on it.

Pinned at 01 · Sales

  1. 01Sales

    A lead walks in, calls, or messages.

    It gets a name, an owner and a follow-up date. No lead sits in a phone nobody checks.

  2. 02Memberships

    The sale becomes a plan, not a promise.

    Start date, price, term and freezes in one record the whole team can see. What was agreed at the desk is what the system enforces.

  3. 03Payments

    Every dinar is tied to the member who paid it.

    Cash, card or transfer, recorded at the desk and reconciled at close. Outstanding balances are visible, not remembered.

  4. 04Reception

    One scan tells the desk everything.

    Active or expired, paid or owing, who is inside right now. The front desk answers in a second instead of calling the owner.

  5. 05Operations

    The day runs on a list, not on memory.

    Shifts, renewals due, freezes ending, tasks assigned. Every task has an owner and a done state, and the manager sees what didn't get done.

  6. 06Members

    See who's slipping before they leave.

    Visits, streaks and absences per member. Reach out while there's still time, with the history in front of you.

  7. Pinned at the bottom, the whole stack moves as one.

    That's RIVET. A sale at the desk, a payment, a check-in and a follow-up are the same record, and every part of the gym lifts together.

02 · Accountability

Every action has a name on it.

Who sold the membership. Who took the cash. Who approved the freeze. RIVET records it as it happens, so end of day isn't an argument and month end isn't an investigation.

Owners see the full picture. Staff see what they need for their job. Nothing gets deleted quietly.

03 · The floor

The desk sees everything the system knows.

Check-ins, expiries, balances, class lists and today's tasks, on the screen the receptionist already has open. When you're not in the building, the gym runs the same way.
