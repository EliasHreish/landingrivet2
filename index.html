Most gyms don’t run on a system. They run on a group chat, a notebook, and a drawer.

  1. 01Sales in a WhatsApp thread
  2. 02Memberships in a notebook at the desk
  3. 03Payments in the drawer

Plate 01 / 06

Sales

From the first walk-in to a signed membership, every step carries the name of the person who handled it.

  • Conversion by staff member

04 A day on RIVET

06:00 Reception

  1. 06:00

    Doors open.

    Members check in. The desk sees who is active, who expires this week and who still owes a balance, before anyone asks.

    Reception · Memberships
  2. 09:30

    A walk-in asks about prices.

    The trial is logged to the staff member who handled it. If they join next week, the sale is theirs, on record.

    Sales
  3. 13:15

    Half now, half next month.

    An installment plan is created, a receipt is issued, and the balance is visible to everyone who needs to see it.

    Payments
  4. 17:30

    Peak hour.

    Class capacity, the trainer schedule and the number of people inside, on one screen at reception.

    Operations · Member activity
  5. 21:00

    Shift handover.

    The cash in the drawer is counted against what the system says was collected. The difference, if there is one, has a name.

    Reception · Payments
  6. 23:00

    Daily close.

    The owner sees the day as it happened: revenue by method, new members, renewals due, and who did what.

    Operations
05 Accountability

Nothing gets edited quietly.

Every sale, payment, check-in and shift change is written down the moment it happens, under the name of the person who did it. Corrections are allowed. Silent ones are not. The owner sees the day as it happened, not as it was remembered.
